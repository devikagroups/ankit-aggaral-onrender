01 / The Profile

The person behind the next chapter.

01

Ankit Aggarwal is the Managing Director of Devika Group, stepping into active leadership in 2007 from the group's corporate headquarters at Devika Tower 6, Nehru Place, New Delhi.

He represents the next generation of a real-estate house whose roots reach back to 1954. His remit spans strategic planning, project execution and expansion across commercial, retail, hospitality and mixed-use development.

Under his leadership, the group has continued strengthening its footing in established markets while opening new ground in emerging corridors, balancing decades of experience with a contemporary approach to urban planning.
